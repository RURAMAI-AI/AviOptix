About AviOptix

Advanced AI-Driven Poultry Consultancy and Optimization Experts

Who We Are

Avioptix is a pioneering poultry consultancy firm founded by Adam Lurie, a seasoned expert with over 25 years of hands-on experience in the poultry sector spanning from Africa to Europe. Adam’s extensive background as a consultant has equipped him with deep insights into diverse poultry operations, from small-scale farms in sub-Saharan Africa to large integrated enterprises in Western Europe.

Co-founded by Kudzayi Matinyarare, who studied medicine in Russia and transitioned into a full-stack developer, this partnership combines Adam’s industry expertise with Kudzayi’s technical prowess in AI and software development, helping us create and scale this innovative business. Drawing on this combined wealth of knowledge, Avioptix was established to revolutionize the industry by integrating artificial intelligence (AI) with traditional poultry expertise.

Our Mission

Independent AI-enhanced poultry supply chain specialists providing cutting-edge consultancy, training, and real-time monetization solutions to the global poultry industry.

Our unique services deliver comprehensive, independent consultancy and training support tailored to the integrated poultry production and manufacturing sectors worldwide. Avioptix incorporates real-time AI data collection and analysis to improve outcomes through customized solutions that detail primary actions, monitor success via hourly AI-driven metrics, and provide ongoing training—whether for Greenfield projects or mature businesses.

We foster strong, long-term client relationships, adapting solutions as your operations evolve, while delivering Capital Investment Projects, Business Improvements, and Transformation Programmes enhanced by predictive AI insights.

Real-Time Monetization

At the core of Avioptix is our commitment to real-time poultry monetization 24/7. Using advanced AI systems, we collect data every hour from farm sensors, genetic tracking tools, and performance monitors. This data aligns directly with genetic performance objectives, such as optimizing broiler growth rates, egg production efficiency, and breed-specific health metrics (e.g., feed conversion ratios and mortality rates).

By analyzing this continuous stream of information, we enable clients to monetize their operations more effectively—identifying cost-saving opportunities, predicting market trends, and maximizing yields in real time.

For Aspiring Entrepreneurs

For aspiring entrepreneurs entering the poultry industry, Avioptix offers detailed business proposals complete with 5-year cash flow projections. These projections are built on robust data models, incorporating variables like initial capital outlay for facilities (e.g., housing, equipment, and biosecurity systems), ongoing operational costs (feed, labor, veterinary care), revenue streams from meat or egg sales, and AI-forecasted adjustments for genetic improvements and market fluctuations.

Our projections assume conservative growth rates (e.g., 5-10% annual yield increase through AI-optimized genetics) and factor in risks like disease outbreaks or feed price volatility, ensuring realistic and actionable plans.

Our Services

Avioptix provides expert production, management, and business development services, augmented by AI technologies for unparalleled precision and efficiency.

Consultancy

Leveraging a global network of highly experienced poultry industry specialists, we offer consultancy grounded in technical, people management, and operational expertise. What sets Avioptix apart is our AI integration:

  • Real-Time Data Collection and Monetization: Our proprietary AI platform collects data 24/7, every hour, from IoT devices monitoring environmental conditions (temperature, humidity, ventilation), animal behavior, and genetic performance indicators. This aligns with objectives such as achieving optimal FCR (Feed Conversion Ratio) of 1.5-1.7 for broilers or hen-day egg production rates exceeding 90%, based on breeds like Ross or Cobb. Clients receive instant insights to monetize operations, such as adjusting feed formulations to reduce costs by up to 15% or predicting flock performance to secure premium pricing.
  • Genetic Performance Optimization: We consult on breed selection and genetic enhancements, using AI to simulate outcomes and align with industry standards. For instance, we help integrate data from genetic suppliers to forecast improvements in traits like disease resistance or growth speed, ensuring compliance with global benchmarks.
